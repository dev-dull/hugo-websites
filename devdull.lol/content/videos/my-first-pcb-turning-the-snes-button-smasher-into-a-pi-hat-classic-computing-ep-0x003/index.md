## Description
In the previous Classic Computing video, I wired a Raspberry Pi to a breadboard full of transistors so it could press the buttons on a real SNES controller. It works, but it is also a rat's nest. So I fired up KiCad to take the Button Smasher 3000 from breadboard to an actual PCB.

I had watched a couple of tutorials so I wouldn't be starting out as a complete idiot. Twelve transistors, twenty-four resistors and twelve LEDs laid out to mimic the buttons on a controller, plus a lot of squinting at a schematic. It took a lot of squinting at a schematic. Then came the part that intimidated me: laying out the board, which may have cost me some of my sanity.
